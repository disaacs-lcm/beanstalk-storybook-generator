Beanstalk Storybook (storybook.applycider.com) makes a personalized "Jack and the Beanstalk" picture book from a photo. It's a student project built for a university AI engineering course. This page explains what the service collects, why, and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. We use them only to sign you in and keep your book attached to your account. We don't get your Google password, and we don't ask for access to Gmail, Drive, Contacts or anything else.
  • The photo you upload. It's sent to OpenAI to draw the book's character reference. We don't save the original photo; only the illustrations made from it are kept.
  • Your book. The illustrations (character reference, cover and pages) and the details you type on the bookplate (name, age and favorite things) are stored so your book is still there when you come back.
  • Logs. The server records each request (time, page, status, your account number and IP address) and each picture it draws (time taken, success or failure). We use these to fix problems, prevent abuse and enforce the hourly picture limit.

Cookies

We use one cookie: the sign-in session cookie that keeps you logged in, for up to 30 days. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, and no tracking across other sites.

Who else handles your data

  • Google handles sign-in.
  • OpenAI draws the illustrations from your photo and the story prompts, through its API. Under OpenAI's API policies, this data isn't used to train its models and may be retained for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring.
  • Render hosts the website and the database where your book is stored.
  • Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS and network protection.

We don't sell or rent your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything besides making and keeping your book.

Photos of children

The book is meant to star a child, so the photo is usually of one. Accounts are for parents, guardians and other adults. Only upload a photo you have the right to use, such as one of your own child or one taken with a parent's permission. The service isn't meant to be used by children under 13 on their own.

How long we keep it

Your book stays saved until you replace it by making a new book, or until you delete your account. Logs are kept for as long as they're useful for running the service. The site is a course project, so the service and everything stored in it may be shut down when the course ends.

Deleting your data

Once you're signed in, use Delete my book and account at the bottom of the left-hand panel. It immediately and permanently removes your account, your illustrations, your bookplate details, and the activity records tied to your account.{% if contact %} You can also email {{ contact }} to ask for deletion.{% endif %} To also revoke the site's access on Google's side, visit your Google account connections.

Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Images are shown only to the signed-in account that made them. No system is perfectly secure, so don't upload anything you wouldn't want stored online.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.
